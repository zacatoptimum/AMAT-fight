## 5. Pricing and comparability rules

Quote each component separately and an all-in reconciliation where possible. Use the issued volume / profile / start / base-date assumptions; provide sensitivity for specified changes. A blank means `NOT PROVIDED`, not zero. Suppliers must identify pass-through items, exclusions, caps / floors, escalation, loss factor, taxes, fees and credit cost. Optimum will normalize for comparison without disclosing one supplier’s confidential offer to another.

## 6. Evidence requirements

Each material claim links to a document / source ID and as-of date. Company capability is not asset availability. Bidder states whether evidence is provided now, available in DD, unavailable or not applicable. Buyer may condition evaluation on verification.
