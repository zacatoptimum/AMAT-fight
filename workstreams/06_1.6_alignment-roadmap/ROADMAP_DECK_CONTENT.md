# Roadmap Deck Content v0.1

## Storyline

把1.x分析結果轉成gates、owners與market authorization；用8頁完成跨部門決策，不重複methodology細節。

## Slide 1 — The roadmap converts analysis into owned decisions and market authority
- Key message：1.x ends only when demand、direction、authority與owners可進入2.x。
- Content：Boundary → Portfolio → Roadmap → Market。
- Visual：four-step pathway。
- Discussion：What must be true before market contact?

## Slide 2 — Three horizons require different decisions, not one monolithic plan
- Key message：Near term closes evidence / authority；medium term tests market / shortlist；long term contracts / implements。
- Visual：three horizon cards。
- Discussion：Which external deadline controls the path?

## Slide 3 — Decision gates define evidence and accountability
- Key message：meetings do not equal gates；each gate needs exit evidence。
- Visual：G3–G5 and G6–G11 gate chain。
- Discussion：Who is accountable for G3 / G4 / G5?

## Slide 4 — Internal readiness can be the critical path
- Key message：data、policy、approval、legal mandate與resources may lead market availability。
- Visual：internal vs external dependency columns。
- Discussion：Which lead time is longest?

## Slide 5 — The first 90 days should use repeated MVP / review cycles
- Key message：each phase produces a reviewable minimum before expansion。
- Visual：five timeboxed MVP rows。
- Discussion：Which review dates are immovable?

## Slide 6 — Market disclosure increases only with explicit authorization
- Key message：desktop → anonymous → named → RFQ；scope expands deliberately。
- Visual：four-step disclosure staircase。
- Discussion：Who approves identity、site、volume、timing與recipients?

## Slide 7 — G5 launch readiness is a concrete checklist
- Key message：portfolio range、questions、fields、boundary、owners、stop rules must be explicit。
- Visual：two-column readiness checklist。
- Discussion：Approve / condition / defer each element。

## Slide 8 — Close with owners, dates and conditions for 2.x
- Key message：the final output is an authorization record, not a generic roadmap。
- Visual：decision table：item / owner / date / status。
- Discussion：Approve / approve with conditions / defer / re-scope。
