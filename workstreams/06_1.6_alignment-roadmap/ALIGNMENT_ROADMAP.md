# Cross-functional Alignment & RE100 Roadmap v0.1

> PRE-KICKOFF DRAFT。AMAT-specific target、timeline、portfolio、owners與approval process均為 `TBD WITH CLIENT`。本文件是對齊與decision architecture，不是承諾 schedule。

## 1. Roadmap intent

Roadmap回答五件事：何時需要什麼decision、需要哪些evidence、誰負責、什麼dependency可能控制critical path、若條件未滿足如何 fallback。它不是一張只有activities與日期的Gantt。

## 2. Stage logic

| Horizon | Objective | Typical actions | Decision output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near term | 鎖定boundary、gap、criteria與market authority | G3 / G4 conditions、source / data closure、desktop scan、anonymous sounding準備 | approved demand / criteria / disclosure ladder |
| Medium term | 驗證供應、條件與shortlist | market scan、engagement、short / long-term assessments、targeted RFQ、DD | comparable offers / shortlist / negotiation mandate |
| Long term | 形成可簽署條件並落地 | commercial alignment、contract review、negotiation、signing readiness、implementation | signed / executable arrangement與BAU governance |

Actual dates與overlap需依 client deadline、market opportunity、data / approval lead time確認。

## 3. Decision gates

| Gate | Decision | Exit evidence | Accountable hypothesis | If not ready |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G0 | Kickoff alignment | charter、RACI、cadence、first data request | Sponsor / Project Lead | condition scope / owners |
| G1 | Data readiness | quality review、gaps、assumption policy | Data / Sustainability owners | range / re-scope |
| G2 | Shared market understanding | workshop decisions、criteria questions | Steering group | targeted follow-up |
| G3 | Baseline accepted | approved gap / range與boundary | Sustainability owner / decision body | scenario-only label |
| G4 | Portfolio direction | scenario trade-offs、constraints、market tests | Steering / Finance / Procurement | retain multiple directions |
| G5 | Market engagement authority | roadmap、scope、disclosure boundary、resources | Sponsor / Procurement / Legal | desktop scan only |
| G6 | RFQ launch | bidder list、package、evaluation rules | Procurement / decision body | further sounding |
| G7 | Shortlist | scorecard、DD、open risks | Decision body | clarify / re-bid |
| G8 | Negotiation mandate | volume、terms、authority、walk-away logic | Authorized approvers | no negotiation commitment |
| G9 | Preferred terms | residual issues、risk acceptance、commercial impact | Approval body | continue / stop / alternative |
| G10 | Signing readiness | final docs、CP、funding、implementation owner | Contract signer / Legal | do not sign |
| G11 | BAU transition | delivery / certificate / reconciliation governance | Operations owner | extended project governance |

Gate owner與authority都是 `HYPOTHESIS / TBD WITH CLIENT`。

## 4. Stakeholder design

- Sponsor：success、resources、escalation、G5 / major gates。
- Sustainability / RE100：target、eligibility、claim / reporting、G3。
- Procurement：market process、disclosure、RFQ、commercial governance。
- Facilities / site energy：load / meter、onsite / wheeling、implementation readiness。
- Finance：cost metric、budget、credit、risk / approval。
- Legal / counsel：NDA、legal advice、contract approval；Optimum不替代律師。
- Regional / global owners：policy consistency、mandate、final approval。
- Optimum：buyer-side method、analysis、market process、commercial advice、records與quality control。

Named people、RACI與availability在kickoff驗證。

## 5. First 90-day planning frame

| Period | First MVP | Review point | Expand only after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | G0、data owner map、workshop / interview plan | sponsor / PM review | decision rights accepted |
| Weeks 2–5 | G1 quality profile、G2 workshop、1.4 boundary MVP | G1 / G2 | material gaps / owners clear |
| Weeks 4–7 | 1.4 gap、1.5 one-year scenarios | G3 / G4 review | baseline / criteria accepted |
| Weeks 6–9 | roadmap、desktop market scan、research log | G5 preparation | disclosure / scope drafted |
| Weeks 8–12 | anonymous sounding或named outreach MVP | market feedback review | written G5 authority |

Dates是planning archetype，不是AMAT承諾。

## 6. Dependency and critical-path tests

每次 status review至少檢查：

- target / policy owner是否已核准；
- consumption / forecast是否reconcile；
- existing procurement / claim evidence是否重複；
- disclosure authorization是否覆蓋identity、site、volume、timing、recipients、use；
- procurement / legal / finance approval lead time是否進schedule；
- external project / COD / wheeling / certificate dependencies是否有evidence與fallback；
- resource / reviewer bottleneck是否把Project Lead拉回Do而無法Review / Decide。

## 7. Transition into 2.x

G5 package至少含：

- approved / conditional portfolio direction與volume range；
- market questions與required comparable fields；
- desktop / anonymous / named disclosure level；
- supplier categories與screening logic；
- contact protocol、confidentiality boundary、no false commitment language；
- owners、review cadence、stop / escalation triggers；
- first MVP：small supplier universe或two-contact sounding，不一次全面接觸。

## 8. Decision / alignment meeting design

會前發一頁：decision、options、evidence、open issues、recommendation / condition。會中只處理需要跨部門取捨的問題；methodology與research放supporting document。會後48小時更新decision / action / assumption / change logs。

## 9. Preliminary roadmap judgement

目前只能合理建議採 gate-based、parallel-evidence approach：在G3 / G4尚有conditions時可進行public desktop scan，但named market outreach需等G5 disclosure / authority。這是 `HYPOTHESIS`，需與AMAT確認deadline與approval reality。

## 10. Definition of launch readiness

進入2.x前：client question、intended decision、portfolio range、information boundary、owner、MVP、review date與stop rule都清楚。若任一項缺失，活動標為conditional而不是默認開始。
